What Happens During Foreclosure in Rhode Island?

When you miss several mortgage payments, your lender will:

  1. Send you default notices
  2. Begin pre-foreclosure proceedings
  3. Schedule a public auction sale of your home

This process can move faster than expected — and once the auction is set, your options become very limited.
