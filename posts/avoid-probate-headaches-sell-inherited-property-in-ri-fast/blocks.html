What Is Probate — and Why Does It Slow Everything Down?

Probate is the legal process that validates a deceased person's will and authorizes the distribution of their estate.

In Rhode Island, probate can take 3 to 9 months (or longer) — especially if:

  • There is no will
  • There are multiple heirs
  • Debts or disputes need to be resolved
  • Paperwork is delayed

During this time, you may still be responsible for the property — taxes, utilities, repairs, and insurance.

That’s why many heirs choose to sell inherited houses quickly instead of dealing with the long, drawn-out process.

Can You Sell an Inherited House Before Probate Is Complete?

✅ Yes — in many cases, you can.

If you're the executor or have legal authority, you may be able to:

  • Accept a cash offer
