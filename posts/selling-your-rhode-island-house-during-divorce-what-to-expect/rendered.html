Who Gets the House in a Divorce in Rhode Island?

Rhode Island is an equitable distribution state, which means the court divides property fairly — not necessarily 50/50.

If the home was purchased together during the marriage, it’s usually considered marital property. That means:

  • Both spouses may be entitled to a portion
  • One can buy the other out
  • Or, the house is sold and the profits split

In many cases, selling the home is the cleanest and least emotionally draining option.

What If Only One Spouse Wants to Sell?

If both names are on the deed or mortgage, both spouses need to agree to the sale. However, if one spouse has full ownership or court-ordered authority, a sale can proceed.
