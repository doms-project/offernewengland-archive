What Are Cash Home Buyers, Really?

A cash home buyer is typically a real estate investor or investment company that buys properties directly — no agents, no lenders, and no waiting on financing.

Benefits include:

  • Faster closings (usually 7–14 days)
  • No repairs or cleaning needed
  • No commissions or hidden fees
  • No showings or open houses

Sounds good, right? It can be — if you’re working with someone reputable.

Red Flags to Watch Out For

Not every “we buy houses” company plays fair. 🚩 Here’s what to avoid:

  • Pushy or aggressive tactics
  • Vague offers like “up to $XXX,XXX” without details
  • Asking you to sign before you understand the terms
  • Out-of-state numbers or generic company names
  • Requests for upfront deposits or inspection fees

Trust your gut — if it feels shady, walk away.
