Can You Sell a Property With Tenants in Rhode Island?
Yes. Under Rhode Island law, you can legally sell a house with tenants — but there are a few rules you need to follow depending on:

  • Whether there’s a lease or month-to-month agreement
  • If you're planning to sell to an investor or an owner-occupant
  • If the tenants are current on rent or causing issues

If the lease is still active, the new buyer will typically inherit the lease. That’s why many landlords prefer selling to cash home buyers in Rhode Island who are used to dealing with tenant-occupied homes.
