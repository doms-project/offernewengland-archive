What Is Considered a Distressed Property?

A distressed home is any property that’s:

  • Physically damaged (roof, foundation, mold, fire, etc.)
  • Facing foreclosure or behind on taxes
  • Vacant, inherited, or occupied by non-paying tenants
  • In need of major renovation or demolition

Most traditional buyers won’t touch these properties — but cash buyers in Providence like Offer New England specialize in buying them as-is.
