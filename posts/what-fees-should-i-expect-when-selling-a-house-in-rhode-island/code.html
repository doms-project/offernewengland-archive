Common Fees When Selling a House in Rhode Island

Let’s break down the typical costs of selling a home the traditional way:

💰 Realtor Commission – 5% to 6%

This is usually split between your agent and the buyer’s agent. On a $350,000 house, that’s $17,500–$21,000.

🏠 Repairs and Updates – Varies ($5,000–$30,000)

Most agents will ask you to fix things like:

  • Outdated kitchens/bathrooms
  • Roof or HVAC issues
  • Paint, flooring, landscaping

🧾 Closing Costs – 1%–3%

This includes title fees, attorney costs, and transfer taxes — often another $3,000–$8,000.

📦 Staging, Cleaning & Holding Costs

Don’t forget expenses like:

  • Professional photos or staging
  • House cleaning
  • Utility bills while the home sits on the market
  • Property taxes during the listing period

👉 It adds up quickly — often $20,000–$40,000+ in total selling costs.

Hidden Fees Most Sellers Don’t Expect

In addition to the obvious charges, many sellers also face:

  • Price drops after inspection
  • Extended holding time (2–3 months average)
  • Last-minute buyer demands
  • Paying both your mortgage and your next rent/home loan while waiting to close
