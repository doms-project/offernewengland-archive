What Happens If You Don’t Pay Property Taxes in RI?

In Rhode Island, if you don’t pay your property taxes:

  1. Your city or town places a tax lien on your home
  2. That lien may be sold to a third-party investor
  3. If unpaid, the investor or town can pursue a tax sale (foreclosure)

You could lose your house even if you don’t owe much — and unlike a traditional mortgage foreclosure, this process can move very quickly.

Can I Sell My House If I’m Behind on Property Taxes?

✅ Yes — you can sell your house with tax debt as long as the property hasn’t already been auctioned off.

In fact, selling before the auction allows you to:

  • Avoid foreclosure and protect your credit
  • Pay off the back taxes from the sale proceeds
  • Walk away with remaining equity
  • Stop legal fees and penalty interest from piling up
