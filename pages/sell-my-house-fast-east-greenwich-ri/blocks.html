We know East Greenwich because we work in it every day

East Greenwich is one of Rhode Island's oldest towns, incorporated in 1677, and the housing reflects it. Selling in a historic town

Old houses, real repair costs, and why an as-is sale often wins

The Kent County Courthouse on Main Street was finished in 1805 and still serves as Town Hall. Plenty of the houses around it are nearly as old. That history is why people love East Greenwich, and it's also why a traditional sale can stall: buyers' inspectors flag knob-and-tube wiring, lead paint, slate roofs, stone foundations, and cesspools, and lenders want them fixed before closing.

A listing in East Greenwich can command a strong price, but only after those items are addressed. If the house needs work you don't want to fund, or you simply don't have the time, an as-is cash sale removes the inspection, the repair negotiation, the appraisal, and the commission from the equation. We look at what the house is worth to us today and put that number in writing.

Will you buy an older home in the East Greenwich Historic District?

Yes. Homes along Main Street, Division Street, and the surrounding Hill & Harbour blocks are often 150 to 250 years old, and we expect the issues that come with that: old wiring, lead paint, slate or wood roofs, stone foundations, and moisture in the basement. None of these stop us from buying. They are priced into the offer, so you do not have to repair or test for anything first.

How do you calculate a cash offer on an East Greenwich house?

We start with what the house would sell for once repaired and updated, using recent East Greenwich sales, then subtract the realistic cost of the work it needs, our holding and closing costs, and a margin that lets us stay in business. The result is written down and explained line by line. There is no obligation to accept it, and no fee for the walkthrough or the offer.

Can I sell an East Greenwich house I inherited before probate is finished?

Usually. In Rhode Island, the executor or administrator needs authority from the probate court before the property can transfer, but we can sign a purchase agreement while that is pending and close once the court paperwork is in place. If the estate has not opened probate yet, we can walk through the timeline with you and your attorney at no cost.

How fast can you close on a house in East Greenwich?

As fast as 14 days once you accept the offer, because we pay cash and do not need a lender, an appraisal, or a financing contingency. If you need longer to move, wait for probate, or coordinate with family, we set the closing date around your schedule. The timeline is yours.

Do I need to clean out or repair the house before you buy it?

No. You can leave the house exactly as it is, including furniture and belongings you do not want to take. We do not ask you to paint, clean, or fix anything, and there is no buyer's inspection to pass. Take what matters to you and we handle the rest after closing.
