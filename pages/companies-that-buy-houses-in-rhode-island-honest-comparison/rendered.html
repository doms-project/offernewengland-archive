Know Your Options

The Four Types of Home Buyer in RI

Most “companies that buy houses” fall into one of these buckets. Each is right for a different seller.

National marketplaces

Sites that collect offers from many investors (Houzeo, Clever, HomeLight and similar). Good for comparing multiple bids — but they’re middlemen, often share your info with several buyers, and the actual purchase still comes from a third party.

Local cash buyers

Companies like Offer New England that buy directly with their own funds. Fast, as-is, no fees, and one local point of contact who knows the RI & MA market. Best when you want speed and certainty without the middleman.

iBuyers

Tech companies that make fast, algorithm-based offers. Convenient for newer, standard homes in good shape — but they charge service fees, deduct repairs, and often won’t touch older or distressed properties common in New England.

A traditional agent

Not a “buyer” but the alternative. Usually nets the most on a fully market-ready home if you can handle repairs, showings, and a 30–60+ day timeline — and pay 5–6% commission.
