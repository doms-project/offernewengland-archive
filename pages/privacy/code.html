Privacy Policy – Offer New England Data Protection

This Privacy Policy outlines how Offer New England , a trusted real estate investment and service company, collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you visit our website.

What information do we collect?

We collect information when you subscribe to our newsletter, complete a form, or share property details with us.

We may gather information about your computer, including IP address, operating system, and browser type using Google Analytics to enhance everyone’s browsing experience. This information doesn’t identify any individual. Learn more about Analytics data collection here.

When ordering or registering on our site, you may be asked to provide your name, email address, mailing address, or phone number. You’re welcome to browse our site anonymously. If Offer New England is acquired by or merged with another company, we’ll transfer your information. In such cases, Offer New England will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

What do we use your information for?

The information we collect from you may be used for the following purposes:

  • To process transactions

Your information, whether public or private, will never be sold, exchanged, transferred, or given to any other company without your consent, except for the express purpose of delivering the requested product or service.

  • To send periodic emails

We may use your email address to send information, respond to inquiries, and/or address other requests or questions you have submitted.

How do we protect your information?

We employ various security measures to protect your personal information when you enter, submit, or access your details through our website.

Do we disclose any information to outside parties?

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This excludes trusted partners who help us operate our website, conduct business, or serve you, provided they keep your information confidential. We may release your information when appropriate to comply with laws, enforce site policies, or protect rights, property, or safety. Non-personally identifiable visitor information may be provided to other parties for marketing, advertising, or other purposes.

Third-party links

Occasionally, we may feature third-party products or services on our website. These third-party sites maintain their own separate and independent privacy policies. We bear no responsibility for the content and activities of these linked sites, though we welcome feedback about them to maintain the integrity of our site.

Third-Party Services
Generally, third-party providers we use will only collect, use and disclose your information as necessary to perform the services they provide to us.

However, certain service providers, such as payment gateways and transaction processors, maintain their own privacy policies regarding information we must provide for your purchase-related transactions.

For these providers, we recommend reviewing their privacy policies to understand how they will handle your personal information.

Remember that some providers may operate in different jurisdictions than you or us. If you proceed with a transaction involving a third-party service provider, your information may become subject to the laws of the jurisdiction(s) where that provider or its facilities are located.

For example, if you’re in Canada and your transaction is processed by a payment gateway in the United States, your personal information used in that transaction may be subject to disclosure under United States legislation, including the Patriot Act.

Once you leave our website or are redirected to a third-party website or application, you’re no longer protected by this Privacy Policy or our Terms of Service.

Remarketing

Offer New England may use third-party vendor remarketing tracking cookies, including but not limited to Google Adwords tracking cookies. This means we may continue showing ads across the internet, specifically on the Google Content Network (GCN). We respect your privacy and do not collect any identifiable information through Google or other third-party remarketing systems.

Third-party vendors, including Facebook and Google, whose services we use will place cookies on web browsers to serve ads based on past visits to our website. This allows us to make special offers and continue marketing our services to those who have shown interest in what we provide.

In addition to cookies and related technologies described above, we may permit certain third-party companies to help tailor advertising that might interest users and collect data about user activities on our Sites and/or Services (e.g., to create targeted ads on third-party services). These companies may deliver ads that might also place cookies and track user behavior.

For example, we may use Google’s “remarketing” service to target our ads to you based on your prior use of our Site when you visit other sites in Google’s content network.

This website may use Google AdWords

This website uses Google AdWords remarketing to advertise on third-party websites (including Google) to previous visitors. We may advertise to previous visitors who haven’t completed a task on our site, such as making an inquiry through our contact form. This could appear as an advertisement on the Google search results page or a site in the Google Display Network. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on someone’s past visits to the Offer New England website. Any data collected will be used according to our privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

California Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

Because we value your privacy, we’ve taken necessary precautions to comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act. We will not distribute your personal information to outside parties without your consent.

Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

We comply with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) requirements and do not collect information from anyone under 13 years of age. Our website, products, and services are directed to people who are at least 13 years old.

Personal, Financial, And Property Information

As a real estate services firm, we may require you to submit certain details to help with your specific situation. This may include contact information, property details, and sometimes financial information needed to analyze your property and circumstances. This information is for our internal use only and won’t be shared with outside parties unless necessary to help resolve your real estate situation.

Online Privacy Policy Only

This online privacy policy applies only to information collected through our website, not to information collected offline.

Changes to our Privacy Policy

If we decide to update our privacy policy, we’ll post those changes on this page.

Contacting Us
