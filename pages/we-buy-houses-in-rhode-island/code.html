How fast can I sell my house in Rhode Island?

In as few as 14 days. Because we pay cash, there’s no mortgage approval or appraisal to wait on. You get a written offer within 24 hours of our visit and pick the closing date that works for you.

What parts of Rhode Island do you buy houses in?

All of it. We buy houses across the entire state — Providence and the urban core, Cranston, Warwick, Pawtucket, the East Bay towns of Bristol and Barrington, Newport and the coast, and South County. Wherever your property is in Rhode Island, we can make a cash offer.

Do I pay any fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions, no listing fees, and no closing costs charged to you. The cash offer we present is the amount you walk away with.

Do I need to make repairs first?

No. We buy Rhode Island houses entirely as-is, in any condition. You don’t have to clean, stage, or fix anything — leave behind whatever you don’t want and we handle the cleanup.

Will you buy a house in foreclosure in Rhode Island?

Yes. We regularly work with Rhode Island homeowners in pre-foreclosure where a fast, certain closing matters most. A cash sale can help you move before the bank takes action.

How do you calculate your cash offer?

We base our offer on your home’s current condition, the cost of repairs needed to bring it to retail-ready, and recent comparable sales in your area. We walk you through the numbers so the offer is transparent — never a lowball surprise.
